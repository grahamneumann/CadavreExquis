A Cross-Disciplinary Arts Series

Theatre 1308 presents Cadavre Exquis, a cross-disciplinary arts series curated by composer and violinist Jeremy Gignoux. Inspired by the surrealist collaborative game "exquisite corpse," this series unfolds as a creative chain reaction: each artist responds only to the work created immediately before theirs.

Music inspires new visual art. Art inspires new music. And the process continues.

Across three events, artists, composers, and ensembles build upon each other's creations. Everything begins with a single musical "seed" created by a mystery composer—their identity will not be revealed until the final event on May 9, 2026.

Part One
"Reflect"
January 17, 2026

The first event reveals the opening link of the artistic chain. Three visual artists debut new works created in response to the original, secret musical recording. Then, original music inspired by those artworks is performed live and recorded—becoming the creative spark for Part 2.

Visual Artists

La Güita

Visual Artist · Storytelling & Muralism
La Güita is a Nicaraguan-Canadian artist based in Calgary/Mohkinstsis whose practice is rooted in storytelling. Guided by memory, emotion, and her connection to the natural and spiritual worlds, her work is an ongoing act of remembering—woven from personal reflections, ancestral teachings, and the stories that cross her path. Using vivid colours, symbolic imagery, and fluid forms, she honours the strength, joy, and sacredness that live within us. Her multidisciplinary practice spans visual art, muralism, and arts facilitation, with murals in Calgary and Michigan through the BUMP Festival, Pleasant Peninsula Mural Festival, and other community initiatives.
@joseelaguita

Wanda Ellerbeck

Visual Artist · Painting, Sculpture & Installation
Born and raised in Alberta, Wanda Ellerbeck's artistic practice has spanned a lifetime, covering painting, drawing, sculpture, and installation. She has taught extensively at post-secondary institutions across Canada and her awards include the prestigious Canada Council for the Arts "B" grant and numerous project grants from the Alberta Foundation for the Arts. She earned her MFA from the Nova Scotia College of Art and Design. Wanda lives, teaches, and works in Canmore, Alberta.
wandaellerbeckartist.com

Terry Gregoraschuk

Visual Artist · Multi-Medium
Terry Gregoraschuk has spent over 45 years exhibiting his work and has developed a reputation for supplying unique art in a variety of mediums and styles. His artwork has been exhibited at the Kennedy Center in Washington, D.C., the National Museum of Natural Sciences in Ottawa, and the Olympic Art Exhibitions in Los Angeles and Calgary. His work is held in the collections of the Alberta Foundation for the Arts, the Medicine Hat Museum, the City of Calgary, and the Esso and Texaco Art Collections.
terrygregoraschuk.com

Part Two
"Improvise"
February 28, 2026

The chain reaction continues. Three visual artists present new works created in response to the music recorded during Part 1. Then, an improvised musical performance responds to the new artworks—recorded and passed forward as the creative catalyst for the final chapter.

Visual Artists

Simon Aitchison

Visual Artist · Oil Painting & Abstract Expressionism
Simon Aitchison is a visual artist based in Calgary, inspired by metaphysics, nature, music and dream-like states. His practice is centered on a lifelong passion for oil painting, a medium he has explored since 1998. He co-founded Vanishing Point Art Gallery (2005–2007) and his work was featured in the Alberta Foundation for the Arts Travelling Exhibition program. His current body of work, the Stone Series, marks a return to his roots—paintings reflecting deep introspection and contemplative thought combined with the action of painting.
simonaitchison.com

Doro Buch

Visual Artist · Oil Painting, Animation & Projection
Doro Buch is a visual artist working with oil painting, animation, projection, and sound. Her work understands the body as a resonant space, where external influences meet inner experience and set things in motion. In her work, form bends toward meaning—line, contour, colour, and composition follow the theme. Abstraction allows simultaneity: different moments in time, reactions, and emotional states existing side by side, held together rather than resolved.
dorobuch.com

Jared Tailfeathers

Multidisciplinary Artist · Blackfoot & Indigenous Art
Jared Tailfeathers is a multidisciplinary artist, musician, author, curator and inventor. His broad variety of work heavily focuses on narrative, community engagement, blending media, genre, high-concepts, public art, design, and public discussions. Much of his work deals with Blackfoot and Indigenous Treaty 7 Art, history, site protection and acknowledgement, Traditional Methods of Making and cultural sustainability.
tailfeathersart.com

Musicians

Jairus Sharif

Experimental Jazz · Saxophone, Synthesizer & Drums
Jairus Sharif is a Canadian experimental jazz musician from Calgary. Starting in music as a hip hop turntablist and DJ, he later played in garage rock and blues bands before discovering avant-garde jazz. After being exposed to the music of South African saxophonist Dudu Pukwana, he bought an alto saxophone and began teaching himself the instrument. His full-length debut Water & Tools (2022) was longlisted for the 2023 Polaris Music Prize. He has toured extensively across Canada, the United States, and Mexico.
